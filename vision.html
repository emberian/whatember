06A · Architectural Philosophy

The Five Pillars of Verifiable Compute

Across breadstuffs, minidregg, nanodregg, zkml-research, degg-research, and obend, five non-negotiable architectural principles govern every component we build.

I

Receipts Are Proof Objects, Not Log Lines

An JSON log entry or a bare signature from an opaque server is an assertion, not a proof. Every external action—whether an LLM inference turn, a proxy tool invocation, or a dataset scan—must emit a self-contained cryptographic certificate binding the pre-state root, the input/output commitment, the policy automaton verdict, and the post-state root.

II

Delete Unverified Mirrors; Export the Theorem Kernel Directly (`@[export]`)

Whenever a system maintains a Lean 4 specification alongside a handwritten Rust/C “mirror” implementation, divergence is inevitable. We systematically delete handwritten Rust mirrors (`ToolGateway` admission, `Orb` HTTP parsing, post-quantum primitives) and link the `@[export]` C-ABI code generated directly by the Lean 4 or HOL4/CakeML compiler.

III

Defense-in-Depth Across Hardware TEEs, Merkle History & Algebraic Proofs

As proven by `residual_trust.rs` (377/1,125 host forgery scenarios under TEE-only signatures), hardware enclaves and zero-knowledge proofs are complements, not substitutes. TEEs provide low-latency confidentiality and hardware identity; MMRs prevent host rollback and split-view equivocation; STARKs and Selvage commit-then-audit prove algebraic and policy invariants independent of silicon vendor microcode.

IV

Structural Anti-Vacuity (`Green Is Not True`)

`0 sorry` is necessary but not sufficient. Every formal specification must pass automated premise-satisfiability and carrier-cardinality censuses (`CarrierCensus.lean`, `ApexPremiseVacuity.lean`), proving that the theorem’s hypotheses are jointly inhabited by real execution traces.

V

Co-Design Algorithms With Silicon Physics (`MXFP4`, `$p_{61}$`, `ExprCAM`, `ICAP`)

Verification only becomes a “capability of the machine, not a tax on it” when the cryptography matches the physical datapath: exact $\le 13$-bit integer dot products for block-32 `MXFP4` weights, degree-64 cyclotomic inertia over $p_{61}$, 8-lane Poseidon2 sponge welding, 8-cycle idle-window taps in Zama’s HPU RTL, and 64-bit hardware CAMs in `obend`.

06C · `~/dev/redregg` & `cv`

Swarm Engineering: How a Multi-Wave Formal Methods Organization Operates

Shipping 1,200+ zero-`sorry` Lean 4 / HOL4 / ACL2 / Isabelle modules alongside 38+ systems repositories required inventing new organizational and context-continuity primitives, documented in ~/dev/redregg and implemented in the cv CLI.

Context Continuity`cv` & `cv distill`

Cross-Vendor Transcript Continuity (`cv-distill-loss`)

The `cv` tool indexes every engineering session across Claude, Codex, and Gemini (`--frozen`), distilling structured handoff states (`GOALS`, `ARTIFACTS`, `NEXT_ACTIONS`). In `~/dev/cv-distill-loss`, we benchmarked multi-hop handoff fidelity, achieving 21.5 / 24 recall across multi-wave proof campaigns.

Organizational LawThe Veterans Rule

The Veterans Rule & Empowered Auditor-Doers

Rather than spawning fresh, context-poor agents that write shallow stubs, waves resume veteran specialists who already hold the module’s invariant map in memory. Every auditor is an Empowered Auditor-Doer: when an audit uncovers a vacuous premise or a broken proof, the auditor fixes the theorem in the same turn.

Cross-Vendor CouncilsAdversarial Review

Reason First, Build Once (`CLAIMS-*.md`)

Every microsite in `dregg-microsites` and every poster in `dregg-posters` is paired with a line-by-line `CLAIMS-*.md` ledger where independent cross-vendor reviewers check every number, commit hash, and theorem name against the live repository before publication.
