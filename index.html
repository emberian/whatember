01A · Executive Thesis

Full-Stack Verifiable Compute

Modern AI infrastructure treats verification as an afterthought—either wrapping an unverified Python proxy inside a single cloud TEE and hoping the operator never equivocates, or attempting naive per-token zkML that collapses at frontier scale. Our work replaces assertion-by-contract with a layered cryptographic and formal stack where every boundary emits a machine-checkable certificate.

“Verification is only a capability of the machine when three independent roots of trust interlock: hardware attestation binds the physical enclave and GPU channel, Merkle / MMR history commitments prevent selective omission or fork equivocation by the proxy operator, and algebraic/STARK + formal kernel certificates prove that the exact model weights, policy automaton, and grammar transitions were obeyed.” — Architectural synthesis across breadstuffs · minidregg · zkml-research · degg-research · obend
Workload & Absence Lean 4 + Plonky3

1. Proving What Did & Did Not Run

How do you prove that a 1.56 TB frontier MoE model ran without silently swapping to an int4 distill—or prove that a training/RAG corpus contains zero instances of a leaked benchmark, PII pattern, or copyrighted span?

We combine Poseidon2/BLAKE3 MMR range certificates (server_cannot_omit_position), 3,522-line Lean 4 Brzozowski regex-complement DFAs (Dregg2/Crypto/Deriv/*), 4M-token/40ms parsing certificates (AttestedAutomatonWeld8.lean), certified BPE tokenization, and Selvage commit-then-audit (AuditSampling.lean).

Merkle Agent Proxies Rust + HOL4/CakeML

2. Proxy Servers with Merkleization for Agents

An agent proxy that logs to Postgres can rewrite history, drop turns, or leak API keys. We built four complementary Merkleization engines: zero-dep BLAKE3 MMR proxies with position-deriving receipts, grain-turn Wasm sandboxes with hardware-wired HISTORY_ROOT_SLOT & HEAP_ROOT_SLOT, 4-ary Poseidon2 Sparse Merkle Trees with non-membership proofs, and zkOracle MPC-TLS outbound HTTPS proxies.

At the bottom sits Orb: a 312-module Lean 4 + HOL4/CakeML/Pancake HTTP proxy dataplane lowered to a verified 1,868-byte x86-64 loop.

3. Hardware Roots, seL4 & obend Silicon

Our pure-Rust tee-verify crate validates AWS Nitro, AMD SEV-SNP (Milan/Genoa/Turin ARK→ASK→VCEK), and Intel TDX DCAP v4/v5 quotes offline, paired with FIPS 203 ML-KEM-768 confidential GPU E2EE (dregg-chutes-e2ee) and a bare-metal seL4 Microkit STARK verifier.

In ~/dev/obend, the proof kernel descends into physical silicon: a 64-bit hardware CAM, 3-phase ICAP dynamic partial reconfiguration (17.89× Verilator speedup), Sky130 PDK DRC proofs, and 6-stage equivalence across FPGA RTL, AMD CDNA3 GPUs, and Ozaki Base-13 FP4 Tensor Cores.
